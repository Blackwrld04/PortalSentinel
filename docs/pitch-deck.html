The Vulnerability 02 / 09

The Multi-Billion Dollar Approval Dilemma

DeFi users routinely sign "Unlimited Allowances" (type(uint256).max) to trade on DEXs and lend in vaults. When a protocol router is compromised, attackers drain user wallets via transferFrom in under 30 seconds.

1. Human Latency Gap

Users are asleep or away from their keyboard. A manual revoke requires noticing the attack, visiting a revoke UI, connecting a wallet, and signing a transaction—taking minutes or hours.

2. Oracle Lag (15-45s)

Decentralized Oracle Networks (Chainlink CRE, etc.) require multi-block Byzantine consensus. By the time nodes agree an attack happened, the attacker already finalized the drain.

3. The ERC-20 Flaw

Standard ERC-20 tokens (like USDC/USDT) cannot be revoked by a third party. Naive revokers silently fail when an attacker drains standard tokens.

Problem: Passive alerts are too late. Active sub-second defense is required.
